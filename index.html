The Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act (15 U.S.C. § 2301) is federal law in the United States governing consumer product warranties. It prevents deceptive warranty practices and enforces implied warranties of merchantability, ensuring goods are fit for their ordinary purpose beyond arbitrary store return windows.
No. Store return policies govern voluntary customer change-of-mind returns. They do NOT negate statutory warranties against manufacturing defects, which typically extend 1 to 4 years under state and federal commercial codes (such as UCC § 2-314 and EU Directive 2019/771).
