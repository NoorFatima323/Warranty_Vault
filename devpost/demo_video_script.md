## Practical Recording Tips
1. **Audio Recording Software:** You can use free tools like **Audacity**, **CapCut**, **Clipchamp**, or record voiceover directly on **Canva** or **OBS**.
2. **Pacing:** Speak steadily without rushing. The script is timed to ~360 words across 158 seconds (~136 words per minute), leaving natural pauses between scene transitions.
3. **Pronunciation Key:**
   - *RMA:* Read as three separate letters: "R - M - A" (Return Merchandise Authorization).
   - *UCC § 2-314:* "U - C - C Section two dash three fourteen".
   - *Magnuson-Moss:* "Mag-nuh-son Moss".
   - *HUD:* "Hud" (Heads-Up Display).
   - *OCR:* "O - C - R" (Optical Character Recognition).
