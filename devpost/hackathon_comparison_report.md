## 1. 🚀 UGREEN Hackathon 2026 on AI NAS: Bring Your Skills to UGOS

### Executive Summary
UGREEN is launching its flagship hardware line of **AI-enabled Network Attached Storage (NAS)** units running **UGOS** (their proprietary Linux operating system designed for local AI processing). Rather than uploading personal media and confidential documents to public cloud servers, UGOS executes AI models locally right on the device inside the user’s house or office. Participants build installable **AI Skills** for the *UGREEN Skills Hub*.

### Prize Breakdown ($46,000 USD Total)
* 🥇 **Gold Award (1st Place):** $20,000 USD
* 🥈 **Silver Award (2nd Place):** $10,000 USD
* 🥉 **Bronze Award (3rd Place):** $6,000 USD
* 💡 **Most Creative Skills Award (2 Winners):** $5,000 USD each ($10,000 total)

### The 4 Competition Tracks
1. **Workflow (Automate Your Workflow):** Automate document sorting, receipt OCR, invoice intelligence, PDF summaries, and scheduled local data backups.
2. **Life (Bring Memories to Life):** Family media management, local semantic photo search, voice memo indexing, and privacy-first digital scrapbooks.
3. **Creativity:** Local generative creation assistants, video/audio editing tools, and content cataloging.
4. **Innovation:** Open-ended prototypes (smart home surveillance, offline edge AI, IoT hardware triggers).

### Submission Process
* **Round 1 (Online):** Submit a Project Proposal (PDF) + Skill Package (ZIP) created via the UGOS Skill Developer Kit.
* **Round 2 (Finalists):** The top 30 finalists receive **free remote cloud access** to physical **UGREEN AI NAS iDX6011 Pro** hardware to test and validate their skill live.

## 2. ⚡ Dakar Bitcoin Hack 2026 by BTrust

### Executive Summary
Organized by **BTrust** (the non-profit Bitcoin development initiative funded by Jack Dorsey and Jay-Z) in conjunction with *Dakar Bitcoin Days*. The objective is to build practical, real-world tools that solve financial hurdles for everyday people across Senegal and Africa.

### Technology Focus
* **Bitcoin & Lightning Network:** Zero-fee, instant micropayments, local point-of-sale systems.
* **Nostr Protocol:** Censorship-resistant peer-to-peer data relays, decentralized identity, and social commerce.
* **AI + Bitcoin:** Autonomous AI agents equipped with Lightning wallets (L402 protocol / Satoshis).
* **Low-Bandwidth Fintech:** SMS/USSD offline transactions tailored for low-spec African devices.
