U.S. Uniform Commercial Code § 2-314 USA FEDERAL / STATE

Mandates that all sold consumer electronics remain fit for their ordinary purpose. Implied warranty remedies extend up to 4 years regardless of 30-day retail store return windows.

Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act (15 U.S.C. § 2301) FEE-SHIFTING REMEDY

Prohibits deceptive warranty disclaimers and grants consumers the right to recover attorney fees and statutory damages when manufacturers fail to honor defect repairs.

EU Directive (EU) 2019/771 EUROPEAN UNION

Provides an automatic mandatory 2-year legal conformity guarantee for any hardware purchased across all 27 EU member states, including free replacement or full reimbursement.
